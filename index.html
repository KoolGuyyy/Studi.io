📊 Percentages

Understand percentages, increases and decreases.

Percentage of a number
Convert the percentage to a decimal, then multiply.

Example:
20% of 80 = 0.20 × 80 = 16

For percentage increase: find the increase, then add it to the original amount.

📐 Pythagoras

Find a missing side of a right-angled triangle.

The formula is:

a² + b² = c²

The longest side is always c. Example: 3² + 4² = 5² 9 + 16 = 25

🧬 Cells

A quick introduction to cell structures.

The nucleus contains genetic information. The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell. Plant cells also have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large vacuole.

⚗️ Atoms

The basic structure of matter.

Atoms contain:

Protons — positive
Neutrons — no charge
Electrons — negative

Protons and neutrons are found in the nucleus.

📚 Essay Structure

A simple structure for organising an essay.

A useful structure is:

Introduction
Introduce your main idea.

Body paragraphs
Explain your points and support them with evidence.

Conclusion
Bring your main ideas together.

🧠 Active Recall

A powerful way to test your memory.

Instead of repeatedly reading your notes, close them and try to remember the information.

For example: "Without looking, can I explain photosynthesis?" Then check your answer and identify what you missed.
